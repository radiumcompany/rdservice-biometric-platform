# RD Service India - Biometric Authentication & RD Services

RD Service India is a leading platform providing secure biometric authentication and Registered Device (RD) services across India. It ensures safe and encrypted identity verification for Aadhaar-based systems.

## About RD Service India

RD Service India specializes in biometric identification and verification technology, offering reliable solutions for fingerprint and iris-based authentication. The platform is designed to meet UIDAI compliance standards and ensure secure data transmission.

It plays a key role in enabling authentication across sectors such as banking, eKYC, government services, telecommunications, and digital identity verification.
